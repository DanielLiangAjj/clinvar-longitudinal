## Verdict

The claim **survives**. Removing somatic-origin variants moves the crude RR from 2.029 to 2.016 
and the adjusted OR from 1.484 to 1.479, because only 7,958 variants (0.22%) carry any somatic-origin 
submission — far too few to generate a 2x contrast. Substituting the germline-appropriate 
'Hereditary Cancer' set for CGC makes the crude effect **larger** (18.18% vs 8.19%, RR 2.220), 
and the adjusted OR stays in a narrow 1.45–1.48 band under every definition.
